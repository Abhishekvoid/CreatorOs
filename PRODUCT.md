# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Indian creators, coaches, consultants and tutors (career coaches, fitness coaches, maths tutors) who currently duct-tape Linktree + Calendly + Topmate + manual UPI collection. Secondary audience: their clients, who land on a creator's public profile from Instagram/WhatsApp and decide in seconds whether to book and pay.

## Product Purpose

CreatorOS is a creator-business operating system: bookings, UPI payments with instant settlement, digital products, client CRM and WhatsApp automation behind one link (creatoros.in/handle), at 0% commission. Success = a visitor lands on a creator's profile and converts into a paid booking or product purchase without friction.

## Brand Personality

Calm, premium, earned-trust. Warm and Indian-market-native (₹, UPI, WhatsApp) without being loud. Confident enough to show real numbers (revenue, ratings, session counts) instead of hype. Voice: plain-spoken, money-respecting ("You keep 100% of what you earn").

## Anti-references

- Linktree: a vertical pile of equal-weight links with no hierarchy, no trust signals, no commerce.
- Generic SaaS gradient-purple landing pages.
- Cluttered creator pages with feeds, community widgets and analytics panels that distract from booking.

## Design Principles

1. **Storefront, not link list.** Every surface moves a visitor through Trust → Interest → Booking → Payment; anything that doesn't is cut.
2. **Show the money honestly.** Real prices in ₹, real metrics, 0%-commission messaging; numbers animate but never exaggerate.
3. **The product demos itself.** Marketing surfaces render believable product UI (dashboard cards, profile phones, booking slots) instead of abstract illustration.
4. **Warm cream, sharp terracotta.** One committed palette (cream/ink with terracotta→amber accents), serif-italic emphasis inside bold sans headlines, film-grain texture.
5. **Motion that earns its place.** Staggered entrance on first fold, scroll reveals elsewhere, full reduced-motion fallback.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG target stated; baseline expectations: visible focus/hover states, `prefers-reduced-motion` fallbacks (already global), semantic landmarks, alt text on portraits, body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast on cream.
